# Code of conduct

Erable follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussions about the project. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated, in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through the contact listed on the [GitHub profile of the repository owner](https://github.com/LePtitDev). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, close discussions or block participants who do not respect these rules.
